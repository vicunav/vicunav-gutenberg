## FAQ

1. `How do I know which package is right for me?` Essential suits a simple presence; Complete suits established or multi-service work; we can decide together.
2. `How long does the process take?` Essential takes about two weeks; Complete takes three to four; the process avoids unnecessary delays.
3. `What do you need from me to get started?` A short preparation checklist; Vicunav guides the rest.
4. `Do I have to write all the content myself?` No; base information is shaped into clear, structured copy.
5. `Can I pay in installments?` Yes; work begins after the first payment.
6. `Do I need to buy my own domain?` Vicunav can help buy and configure one or connect an existing domain.
7. `Do I need to arrange my own hosting?` No; it is included in monthly maintenance.
8. `What does the maintenance plan include?` Hosting, security, updates, backups and technical maintenance.
9. `What happens if I cancel the maintenance plan?` Vicunav stops hosting and managing the site and can help with transition.
10. `Who owns my website?` The client owns the site and all its content.
11. `Will my website be optimized for search engines?` Every site includes basic SEO structure and configuration.
12. `Can my website help me appear in AI tools like ChatGPT?` Clear structure and consistent information help search and AI systems understand the business.
13. `Will I be able to update my website myself?` Yes; a post-launch walkthrough supports small changes.
14. `Can I request additional changes after launch?` Small adjustments are maintained monthly; larger work is scoped separately.
15. `What counts as a small adjustment and what is a major change?` Copy and image edits are small; new sections, structure or functionality are larger and estimated first.
16. `Can you redesign my current website?` Yes; Vicunav can rebuild or restructure existing content.
17. `Do you work with clients outside Latin America?` Yes; work is remote in Spanish and English.
