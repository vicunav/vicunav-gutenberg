## Situations

Título: `Do any of these situations sound familiar?`

1. Your website does not reflect the true quality of your work. It looks more generic than what you actually offer.
2. Explaining your services in writing is harder than it seems. You are never quite sure what to say or how to organize it.
3. Your ideal clients cannot find you on Google, and when someone asks ChatGPT for a professional like you, you do not appear.
4. The technical side, your domain, hosting, and updates, takes time away from your business.
5. People visit your website but do not contact you. Something holds them back, and you do not know exactly what it is.
6. You want something professional that builds trust, without sounding corporate or disconnected from your work.
7. Your clients still have to call or message you to book. There is no clear way to schedule from your website.
8. You use too many separate tools to run your business, and nothing is connected.
